## 🌟 Why Postbox Reborn Exists 🧭

There was a moment, somewhere between the rise of webmail tabs and the decline of serious desktop clients, when email felt like a place you *visited* rather than *worked*. Postbox Reborn is a love letter to the alternative idea: that a mail client should feel like a well-organized desk, with drawers that open precisely, labels that stick, and a chair that never squeaks.

We rebuilt the experience around three stubborn requirements:

1. **Speed at the speed of thought.** Opening a 40,000-message archive should feel like flipping a page, not waiting for a weather report.
2. **Search that actually finds things.** Not "best guess" search — a precise, filter-first query engine that speaks your language.
3. **A UI that respects your attention.** No animated upsells, no pop-up tours, no nag screens. Just mail.

If Mailbird and the original Postbox were two siblings, this repository is the cousin who quietly went to engineering school and came back with something better.

## 🧩 Feature Landscape

### Core Mail Engine ⚙️

- **Unified Inbox Hub** — Gmail, Outlook, IMAP, POP3, Exchange, and generic SMTP all feed into one calm stream. Tabs per account keep personas separate when you need them to be.
- **Conversation Threading** — Mute, ignore, or "snooze a thread" until the people inside it calm down. Threads collapse intelligently without hiding context.
- **Native Search Query Language** — Compose filters like `from:acme after:2026-01-01 has:attachment` and get exact matches in milliseconds, even across a decade of mail.
- **Deferred Send** — Schedule a message for a future moment, then edit it silently before it departs. Excellent for anyone working across time zones.
- **Follow-Up Watch** — Mark a sent message "nudge me if no reply by Friday." The client politely taps you on the shoulder.
- **Offline-First Storage** — Full mailbox mirroring means you work in a cabin with no Wi-Fi and everything still opens instantly.

### Productivity Layer 🧠

- **Quick Actions Bar** — Archive, label, reply, forward, and mute from a single toolbar that adapts to the current selection.
- **Saved Replies & Snippets** — Canned responses with variable support for names, dates, and invoice numbers.
- **Inbox Triage Mode** — A focused keyboard-driven sweep that lets you clear 200 messages in under a minute.
- **Calendar Peek** — A side panel surfaces upcoming events pulled from connected accounts, so meeting invitations don't hide in the noise.
- **Task Extraction** — Turn any email into a task card. Tasks carry a due date, a priority, and a link back to the source thread.

### Search & Organization 🔍

- **Faceted Filter Rail** — Narrow by sender, domain, attachment type, read state, and date range simultaneously.
- **Smart Folders** — Persistent queries that behave like conventional folders without moving a single byte.
- **Tag Stacking** — Apply multiple tags to one message; the client remembers the order.
- **Duplicate Collapser** — Finds and merges near-identical messages across accounts.
- **Attachment Vault** — Every file you've ever received, extracted and indexed, searchable by extension, size, and sender.

### Interface & Experience 🎨

- **Responsive UI** — The layout flexes gracefully from a 1366×768 laptop panel up to an ultrawide 5K workstation. Panes fold, collapse, and expand without breaking alignment.
- **Multilingual Support** — 31 interface localizations shipped in-box, with right-to-left rendering for Arabic and Hebrew.
- **Light, Dark, and Sepia Themes** — A calming palette inspired by paper, ink, and dusk.
- **Keyboard-First Navigation** — Every action is reachable without a mouse. A full shortcut sheet is built into the help menu.
- **Tabbed Composition** — Write three replies at once; each draft lives in its own tab.
- **HiDPI & Fractional Scaling Ready** — Crisp text on 125%, 150%, and 175% display scaling.

### Security & Privacy 🔐

- **OAuth 2.0 Token Flow** — Modern auth for Gmail and Outlook accounts.
- **S/MIME & PGP Support** — Encrypt and sign with your existing keyring.
- **Remote Content Guard** — Tracking pixels stay blocked until you allow them.
- **Encrypted Local Vault** — Mailbox cache is sealed at rest with OS-level protections.
- **No Telemetry by Default** — Nothing leaves your machine unless you explicitly opt in.
- **Phishing Heuristics Panel** — Suspicious links get a warning banner with a hover-preview of the true destination.

### Integration & Extensibility 🔌

- **Native Windows Notifications** — Toast alerts that respect Focus Assist.
- **Jump List & Taskbar Badges** — Direct access to compose from the taskbar.
- **Command-Line Launcher** — Open a specific mailbox or compose window from a shortcut.
- **Portable Profile Export** — Move your entire configuration to a new machine in one archive.
- **Plugin Bridge API** — A documented hook system for community add-ons.

## 🚀 First Launch Walkthrough 🗺️

Postbox Reborn greets you with a calm setup wizard — three steps, no account upsells.

1. **Add Your First Account.** Choose from Gmail, Outlook, or a generic IMAP preset. OAuth handles the dance for you on modern providers.
2. **Pick a Layout.** Classic three-pane, widescreen two-pane, or "focus mode" (a single pane with a transient reading view).
3. **Set Your Sweep Style.** Decide what happens when you archive: does it move to All Mail, a local archive, or a Smart Folder?

After that, the client quietly syncs in the background and your inbox appears within seconds of a healthy connection.

## 🧭 Common Workflows

### The Morning Sweep ☕
Open the client, press `J` to jump into the unified inbox, and sweep with `E` (archive) or `H` (snooze). The Follow-Up Watch pane shows replies you're waiting on.

### The Invoice Trail 🧾
Create a Smart Folder named "Invoices 2026" with the query `has:attachment after:2026-01-01 subject:invoice`. Every incoming invoice lands there automatically — no dragging required.

### The Multi-Persona Day 🎭
Freelancers and small teams run several identities side by side. Tabs per account keep work, personal, and side-project mail neatly separated. The unified view stays available for the rare moment you actually want everything at once.

### The Offline Flight ✈️
Full mailbox mirroring means you can triage, reply, and organize during a six-hour flight. On landing, the client quietly pushes everything out in the correct order.
